# Yunxiang Wei

Undergraduate student at **Zhejiang University** working on **code LLMs**, **agents**, and **reliable AI systems**.

My research focuses on making language models more dependable in real-world tasks such as coding and scientific discovery. In coding, I care about generated-code reliability, including data-risk detection and correctness estimation or verification. In scientific discovery, I am interested in equipping AI systems with interdisciplinary knowledge for idea generation, evaluation, and verification.

My long-term goal is to build automated systems with continual knowledge learning and strong reliability.
